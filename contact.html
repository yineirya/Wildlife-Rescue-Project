Frequently Asked Questions

What should I bring when visiting?

Comfortable shoes, water bottles, and a camera to capture memories.

How can I volunteer?

Fill out the volunteer application on our website or contact us at volunteer@wildlifesanctuary.org.

Are pets allowed?

No, pets are not allowed for the safety of our wildlife.

Do you offer group tours?

Yes, group tours can be arranged by contacting info@wildlifesanctuary.org.

How can I donate?

You can donate online via our donation page or contact donations@wildlifesanctuary.org.
