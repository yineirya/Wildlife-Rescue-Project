Who We Are

Wildlife Sanctuary is a wildlife rescue NGO dedicated to the preservation and safety of animals. We provide a safe haven for animals that have been injured or orphaned, and we work to rehabilitate them so they can be released back into the wild. Our mission is to protect and preserve the natural world, and we are committed to educating the public about the importance of conservation and the need to protect our planet's biodiversity.

Interested?

We are always looking for passionate, dedicated volunteers who share our love for animals and want to make a real difference. Whether you're assisting with animal care, helping with rescues, participating in outreach programs, or supporting our administration, your time and effort will have a direct impact on the lives of the animals we serve.

Interested in making a difference? Reach out today to learn more about how you can volunteer, support our mission, and connect with professionals in the environmental industry. Together, we can help protect and preserve the wildlife that share our world: we've only got one planet!
